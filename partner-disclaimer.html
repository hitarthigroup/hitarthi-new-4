Earnings Disclaimer (English)
HITARTHI does not guarantee any income. Earnings of a Team Partner depend only on genuine sales of HITARTHI products (courses, e-books, books and health products) to real customers, and on the partner's own effort and skill. There is no joining fee, and no income is paid merely for recruiting people. Levels and positions are never sold. Any figures shown on this website (calculators, examples, reward targets) are illustrations only, not promises or averages. Partners must not make income claims to others. HITARTHI follows the Consumer Protection (Direct Selling) Rules, 2021.
